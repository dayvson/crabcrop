A JPEG with a `fill` flattens an image with transparency over the fill in one pass (issue #808). The flatten built a canvas of the fill, copied the image to RGBA8, blended it over the canvas a pixel at a time through the image crate's pixel accessors, and converted the canvas to RGB8: four passes and three buffers. Each pixel is now blended over the fill by the crate's own arithmetic, in its order, straight into the RGB8 result. An 800x533 cut-out flattens in 0.83 ms where it took 2.31 ms, and a 2400x1600 one in 10.8 ms where it took 28.1 ms. Responses are unchanged.
