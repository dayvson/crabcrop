Tags, embeddings, the aesthetic score and moderation resample only the
224x224 crop their models read (issue #673). The preprocessing those sections
share resized the whole render with the image crate's bicubic resize, so that
its short side was 224, then kept the center 224x224 and dropped the rest.
The crate reads one pixel at a time into a float image, on one thread: that
resize was 6.9 of the 7.2 ms the preprocessing took on an 800x533 render and
32 ms on an unresized 2400x1600 one, paid again by each of those sections a
meta request asks for. Only the crop's window is now resampled, a row at a
time, with the crate's arithmetic: the same weights, the same order of
additions for every sample, the same clamp and rounding. The preprocessing
went from 6.3 to 1.4 ms on the 800x533 render and from 32 to 6.0 ms on the
2400x1600 one. RGB8 and RGBA8 renders take the new path and every other
layout the crate's; the cap on the long side of an extreme aspect is applied
first, as before. The model's input is the same bit for bit, so every score
and embedding is the same. Responses are unchanged.
