`/metrics` shows the request log's queue and the AI, video, animation and
`/explain` probe gates (issue #707). The queue: the bytes of lines it holds,
`crabcrop_request_log_queue_bytes`, against the 8 MiB it holds before requests
wait for room, `crabcrop_request_log_queue_capacity_bytes`; the requests
waiting for room, `crabcrop_request_log_requests_waiting`; and the seconds it
has spent full, `crabcrop_request_log_queue_full_seconds_total`. With
`CRABCROP_LOG_SYNC=1` there is no queue, and none of these series. The gates
are shown as the lanes are, `crabcrop_gate_in_use` against
`crabcrop_gate_capacity`, with `gate="ai"`, `gate="video"`,
`gate="animation"` and `gate="probe"`. The loader and engine error counters,
the byte totals and the cache hit and miss counters gained the `# HELP` lines
they lacked, so every series now has one.
