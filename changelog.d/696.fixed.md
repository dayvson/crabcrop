JPEG XL output of a small image with an alpha channel, at a quality under
15, no longer decodes fully transparent (issue #696). The encoder coded
alpha as lossily as the quality asked, and under 15 it could round away the
whole alpha channel of a frame this small: the functional corpus's cut-out,
rendered at every size from 1x1 to 40x40, decoded with every pixel clear at
168 sizes of up to 256 pixels, 16x16 and 32x8 among them, and at 16x8 it did
at every quality from 1 to 12. Under quality 15 the alpha of a frame of up
to 4096 pixels (64x64) is now coded as at quality 15, while its color still
follows the quality asked for, and no size of that sweep decodes fully
transparent under 15. This can change the bytes of JPEG XL output with
an alpha channel under quality 15 of a frame that size, the encodes a
`max_bytes` budget steps down to included: of the 490 encodes of the 35
small frames of the test fixtures under 15, 252 change, and all 490 come out
1.9% larger in total, 3.6% at quality 1 and 0.4% at 14. Output at quality 15
and up, output of a larger frame and output without alpha keep their bytes.
The cache epoch above covers the change.
