Changelog entries are now files of their own (issue #539). A pull request
adds `changelog.d/<issue>.<section>.md`, holding its entry's paragraph,
instead of a line in CHANGELOG.md, so open pull requests no longer fall into
conflict on CHANGELOG.md each time one of them merges. `scripts/bump_version.py`
writes the release's section from the `## [Unreleased]` lines and the
fragments, which `scripts/assemble_changelog.py --check` previews, and the
changelog check accepts a fragment as the entry and refuses one the release
could not assemble. A line under `## [Unreleased]` still counts.
CONTRIBUTING.md describes the format.
