The blurred backdrop of `pad:WxH:blur` is resampled only where the canvas shows it (issue #806). The backdrop is the image scaled with the Triangle filter to cover the canvas, then cut to the canvas. The image crate scaled the whole image, a pixel at a time in floats, and then cut it; an RGB8 or RGBA8 image now has only the part the canvas keeps computed, over rows, with the crate's weights, sums and rounding (as for the CLIP crop, issue #673). The cover of a 2400x1600 photograph for an 800x800 canvas went from 41.0 to 8.6 ms, and `pad:800x800:blur` of a 2400x1600 JPEG from 124 to 90 ms end to end. Other layouts take the crate's resize. Responses are unchanged.
