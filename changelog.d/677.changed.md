The face model and the object detectors remember their recent detections
(issue #677). The engine shares one detection between the operations of a
request, but nothing was shared between requests: `crop:400x300:smart`,
`crop:800x600:smart` and `crop:1200x630:smart` of one photo fed the face
model the same 640x640 input three times, and the objects crop and the
class filters did the same to the detector, because each size is its own
miss in the result cache. Each provider now keeps the boxes of its 256
most recent inputs, keyed by a 128-bit digest of the frame the model is
fed, the source size the boxes map back to and, for a detector, its score
floor and class names. The digest is SipHash-2-4 under a secret each
provider draws when it loads, so nobody can make two inputs share a key.
Over the face model's input it took 0.57 ms with or without the CPU's SHA
extensions, where a SHA-256 took 0.79 ms with them and 3.7 ms without. A
repeat is answered without running the model: the focal chain of a smart
crop on a 1400x700 group photo went from 7.0 to 2.2 ms and a detection on
an 810x1080 photo from 41.3 to 1.9 ms. A first-time input pays for the
digest: that focal chain went from 6.8 to 7.1 ms, and the detection
stayed inside its run-to-run spread. The memo holds keys and boxes, never
pixels, at most 64 boxes for an entry, and lives in the provider: a
restart empties it. A failed detection is never remembered, and a repeat
returns the boxes a fresh run would. Responses are unchanged.
