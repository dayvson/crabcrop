Class redaction with a segmentation model runs at most 64 segmentation decodes in a request, and checks the processing limit before each one (issue #713). Each detection of `blurclass`, `pixelateclass` or `recolorclass` costs one decode, and nothing bounded them: a crowd ran a decode per person (an evaluation measured about 1.2 s more for 35 more people in a debug build) while the request held one of the AI permits, and nothing between the decodes checked the deadline. The detections past the 64th, in the detector's order (most confident first), are now redacted by their rectangles, as without the model; a rectangle holds the whole object, so nothing is left unredacted, and the meta `segments` object lists the objects that were segmented. Requests with 64 detections or fewer render as before.
