A pod whose AI models took more than about 45 seconds to load was restarted before it served (issue #715). The server binds its port before it loads its models and answers nothing, `/health` included, until boot is done, and the Helm chart and the kustomize base had only liveness and readiness probes, the first at 5 seconds and a restart after three misses 20 seconds apart. Both now declare a startup probe on `/health` every 2 seconds, which holds the other two until it first succeeds and restarts the pod after 150 misses in a row: 5 minutes. The chart reads the numbers from `startupProbe.periodSeconds` and `startupProbe.failureThreshold`, with those defaults for values an upgrade with `--reuse-values` keeps. The images' `HEALTHCHECK` has a 5-minute start period, so its misses while the server boots do not count against the container; the AI image inherits it.
