Group write-backs to the shared tier no longer pile up while the bucket stalls (issue #709). A hit that adds a cache group to a stored entry writes the bucket copy in the background, and with all 64 write slots taken each such copy waited for one on a task of its own, holding the entry, with no limit. At most 64 now wait, and past them a write-back is skipped and counted in `crabcrop_cache_shared_group_writes_skipped_total`, as a render's write-through already was, next to `crabcrop_cache_shared_group_writes_waiting`. A skipped write-back leaves the bucket copy without the group until a later hit sends it: with a disk tier holding the entry, that replica's next disk hit does. A group purge meanwhile does not reach that bucket copy: purge its URL, which does. A large bucket write, an entry or an upload, also no longer holds a runtime worker while its body is serialized and hashed: both run on the blocking pool, with the same digest.
