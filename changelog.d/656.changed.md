A pad no longer clones the frame or blends opaque pixels one at a time
(issue #656). `pad:WxH:<fill>` cloned the whole frame before fitting it,
though only the blurred fill reads the frame again; built its canvas and
then copied it; and placed the fitted image through the image crate's
overlay, which converted it to RGBA8 and blended it pixel by pixel, though
an opaque pixel over anything is that pixel. The frame is now cloned for
the blurred fill only, the canvas is built once, and a fitted image without
an alpha channel is written over the canvas by rows. `pad:800x800:ffffff`
of a 2400x1600 JPEG went from 12.4 to 5.3 ms in the pipeline, and a solid
fill holds one frame less at its peak. A fitted image with alpha is blended
as before. Responses are unchanged.
