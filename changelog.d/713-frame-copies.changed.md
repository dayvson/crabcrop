Animated renders copy their frames less (issue #713). A rendered frame was copied into the frame list even when it already was RGBA8, the first frame was copied for the `quality(auto)` pick of animated WebP output and again for the placeholder, and the trim survey of issue #465 copied every frame for every trim. A rendered frame now moves into the list, the first frame is lent to the quality pick and the placeholder, and a trim that is the chain's first step reads the frames where they are; a trim after other steps still runs them on a copy of each frame. Responses are unchanged: through 37 operation chains, an animated GIF and an animated WebP rendered the bytes they did wherever no other change of this release reached them.
