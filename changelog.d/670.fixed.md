JPEG XL output of a small image with an alpha channel no longer answers
`500` (issue #670). The encoder, jixel 0.5.5, panicked on some frames with
transparency from 8x8 to 25x25 pixels and on thin strips such as 9x17, 1x33
and 2x64: a thumbnail or an icon cut from a transparent PNG. Of 280 renders
of the functional corpus's cut-out at 56 sizes from 1x1 to 128x128 and five
qualities, 69 answered `500 image processing failed`. The panic was
contained, so later requests were served, and a client whose `Accept` header
selects JPEG XL met it without naming the format. The lock moves to jixel
0.5.7, which carries the upstream fix from 0.5.6, and all 280 answer `200`
with an image that decodes to the frame's size with its alpha channel. At
the default speed tier the other JPEG XL outputs keep their bytes, except a
few small frames with alpha; at `CRABCROP_JXL_SPEED=fast` and the slower
tiers every JPEG XL output gets new bytes, within about 1% of the old size
in total. The cache epoch above covers both. At a quality under 15 the
encoder could still drop the whole alpha channel of a frame this small,
which then decoded fully transparent, as 0.5.5 already did for 13x13 at
quality 5: the fix for issue #696 codes the alpha of such a frame as at
quality 15. The suite now encodes small frames with transparency in every
output format and decodes each one back.
