A meta response's placeholder computes its four resizes over rows (issue #811). The dominant color, the preview and the palette came from the image crate's Triangle resize and the ThumbHash from its Lanczos3 resize, which read one pixel at a time into float images. They now come from the row resamplers that already stand for those resizes byte for byte: the CLIP crop's (issue #673) for the three Triangle ones, and the scale of `overlay(...,width)` for the ThumbHash. For an 800x533 render the four took 10.7 ms and take 3.2 ms, and a meta request with placeholders for `800x0` of a 2400x1600 JPEG went from 22.8 to 14.8 ms. The placeholder is unchanged.
