A request's future no longer carries the cache-miss path (issue #638). A
future is as large as the largest thing it may await, and the image route
awaited the miss path in line, so every image request allocated and moved a
24 KB future sized for the source fetch and the render: a memory hit, a
`304` and a `403` as much as a miss. The miss path is now boxed where a
request reaches it, and the future is about 5 KB. A memory-tier hit,
measured in process on the real router with no sockets (signed URLs, a
browser's request headers, a 20 KB body), went from 7.77 to 7.04 µs. A miss
makes one more small allocation, next to its fetch and its render.
Responses, headers, cache keys, log lines and metrics are unchanged.
