The AI models read the frame where it is (issue #672). Every provider that
resizes its input (the face model behind `crop:...:smart`, the mask model
behind `removebg` and `studio`, the object detector, the segmenter and the OCR
detector) copied the whole frame twice first: once to get RGB8 pixels, also
when the frame already was RGB8, as every decoded JPEG is, and once more to
hand the resizer an image it owned. For a 2400x1600 source that is two
11.5 MB buffers in front of a face model that answers in 5 ms. The resizer
now borrows the frame, and a frame in another layout is converted once, as
before. The face model's input is filled a plane at a time, and `removebg`
writes the cut-out of an opaque RGB8 source in one pass. With the face model
on, the focal chain of a smart crop on a 2400x1600 source went from 18.9 to
15.2 ms, and the detections of a meta response on the same frame from 70 to
58 ms. Every model is fed the same values bit for bit, so masks, boxes and
focal points are the same. Responses are unchanged.
