In the `crabcrop-url` crate, `SealedUrl` builds a sealed URL from named parts
(issue #712): `SealedUrl::new(base, operations, source)`, then `expires`,
`name` and `key_id` as the URL needs them, and `build(secret)`. It takes the
secret itself, so it cannot fail for want of one, and it answers what
`build_sealed_url` answers for the same inputs.
