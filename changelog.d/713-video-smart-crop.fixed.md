A smart crop holds still across the frames of a video (issue #713). GIF and WebP output of an animation decide a smart crop on the first frame and cut every frame from that window (issue #376), but MP4 and WebM output let each frame decide for itself, so the crop followed the detail and jittered from frame to frame, and the focal analysis ran once per frame. Video output now shares one window per crop, as the other animated outputs do.
