A progressive JPEG source decodes at full size again, whatever the request (issue #713). Since issue #653 a JPEG whose first operation is a resize to a third of its size or less is decoded at reduced scale by libjpeg-turbo, and the full-size decoder, zune-jpeg, reads every other request. The two part on some damaged progressive files: libjpeg-turbo reads their scans without a warning where zune-jpeg refuses them (9 of 42,000 mutated files in the differential run behind issue #653, all progressive), so such a file rendered at small sizes and failed at large ones. No header shows the damage, and learning zune-jpeg's answer means its full decode, so a progressive file now always gets it, at every size: the picture, or the `502`. Baseline files keep the scaled decode. Resizes of progressive JPEGs give up its speed: issue #653 measured a 2400x1600 progressive photograph resized to 800 wide at 30.9 ms through the scaled decode and 46.1 ms at full size. Their pixels change back to the full-size decode's.
