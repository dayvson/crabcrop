`studio(product)` composites a row at a time (issue #674). Once the mask
model has answered, the composite found the subject's box, cleaned its
edge, scaled it, cast its shadow and laid it on the backdrop one pixel at
a time, through the image crate's pixel accessors or routines built on
them: 50 ms on a 1100x733 product shot, next to the model's 131 ms. The
steps now work on rows. The box comes from the first and last covered
pixel of each row. The edge cleanup works on the cut-out's own buffer and
computes each grid cell's background once. The subject is scaled by the
crate's Triangle resize done over rows. The shadow is written straight
onto the plain backdrop from the 256 shades the crate's blend gives, with
no canvas-sized layer in between, and the subject overlay copies opaque
pixels, skips transparent ones and hands the rest to the crate's blend.
The composite went from 50.5 to 25.7 ms on that shot, and
`filters:studio(product,bg:white)` with the model from about 178 to
about 160 ms. The image and the studio record are the same byte for byte.
Responses are unchanged.
