`/health` answers `503` with the code `request_log_stalled` once the request
log's queue has stayed full for 5 seconds, and `200 ok` again once the queue
has room (issue #707). Since issue #642 an image request, a purge or an upload
waits while the queue is full, so when nothing read standard output (a blocking
log driver whose sink is down, a hung journald, a stuck log shipper) the
replica answered none of them while `/health` kept answering `200`, and the
liveness probes in the Helm chart and the kustomize base never restarted it, as
they did through 0.12, when such a standard output held the whole server, the
health check included. The check reads two atomics and never waits on the
queue; the requests go on waiting, and no line is dropped. A standard output
that is read takes the whole queue in well under a second, and 5 seconds is as
long as a request waits for a processing slot or for source buffer budget
before it is shed, so a short pause in the reader does not fail the check, and
those liveness probes restart a stalled replica 45 to 65 seconds after its
queue filled. `crabcrop health`, the images' `HEALTHCHECK`, fails on the `503` too.
Two series change with it. An image request's time now runs from its arrival,
its wait for room in the queue included, in its line's `total_ms` and in
`crabcrop_request_duration_ms`, which left the wait out. And
`crabcrop_requests_total` counts a `304` revalidation under a new
`status_class="3xx"`, where it counted as `other`.
