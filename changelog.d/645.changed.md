The memory tier finds the entry to evict without reading every entry (issue
#645). To evict, the tier read the last-use stamp of every entry it held,
once for each entry evicted, under its one lock. A full tier evicts on
every miss, so each miss walked the whole tier while hits on other workers
waited. The keys are now kept in order of last use, and eviction takes the
first. A put that evicts went from 28.2 to 0.85 µs with 13,000 entries of
20 KB (the default 256 MiB full of 800-pixel renders) and from 211.9 to
1.38 µs with 72,000 thumbnails. A hit costs about 50 ns more, under 1% of a
memory hit. Which entry is evicted and when, what an entry is charged
against `CRABCROP_CACHE_MAX_MB`, and every response are unchanged.
