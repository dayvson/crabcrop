The shutdown line for the requests a drain cuts off now counts them (issue #715): `shutdown: 2 requests still in flight at the deadline are cut off`, where it read `shutdown: requests still in flight at the deadline are cut off`, so an alert on the old text needs the new one. A request counts while its handler runs or its response is still being sent. A second signal that ends the drain reports them the same way, `at the second signal`, where nothing counted them. The grace itself is unchanged: `CRABCROP_SHUTDOWN_GRACE_SECS`, 8 seconds by default. In the library, `crabcrop::server::serve_connections` takes the count, the new `crabcrop::server::RequestsInFlight`, as its last argument.
