Filters whose arguments change nothing are skipped (issue #810). `brighten(0)`, `gamma(1)` and `exposure(0)`, whose tables map every value to itself, `saturation(1)`, `huerotate` by a whole number of turns and `rgb(0,0,0)` ran over every pixel and left each as it was. At 800x533 they cost 0.35 to 2.2 ms each; skipped, each takes a few microseconds at most, and `800x0/filters:saturation(1):huerotate(0)` of a 2400x1600 JPEG went from 14.7 to 12.2 ms end to end. They are skipped on RGB8 and RGBA8 images, and `brighten(0)` on 8-bit gray ones too. On other layouts they run as before, since all of them but `brighten` and `huerotate` turn the image into RGB8 or RGBA8 there. `contrast(0)` still runs, since its table moves the values 1 to 63 down a level (issue #815). Responses are unchanged.
