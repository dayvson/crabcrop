`contrast`, `sharpen`, `saturation`, `vibrance`, `equalize` and `enhance` filter an image with transparency where its pixels lie (issue #812). They ran on an RGB8 copy of the image, whose colors were then copied back into the image's RGBA8 form one pixel at a time; they now run on the RGBA8 form and leave its alpha as it is, as the other color filters do. At 800x533, `contrast(20)` of an RGBA8 image went from 0.93 to 0.37 ms, as fast as on RGB8; `saturation`, `vibrance`, `equalize` and `enhance` are 1.24 to 1.45 times as fast, and `sharpen` 1.1 times. Responses are unchanged.
