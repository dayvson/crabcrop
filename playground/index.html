crabcrop playground

Build, sign, preview, and debug crabcrop URLs entirely in your browser. Your secret never leaves this page: signing happens locally with WebCrypto, the secret is never stored and never sent to any server, and the only network requests this page makes are to the crabcrop endpoint and image sources you enter. The scheme is the one in spec/url-signing.md.
